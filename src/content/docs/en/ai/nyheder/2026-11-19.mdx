### 4. Google AI Blog: The Gemini app is now available on lower-memory Android devices.

**What changed:** Google AI Blog published the update on 18 Nov 2026. The Gemini app is now available on lower-memory Android devices, specifically eligible Android &#40;Go edition&#41; devices.
