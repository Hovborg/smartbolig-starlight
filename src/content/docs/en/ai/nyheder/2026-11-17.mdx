### 5. Google DeepMind Blog: Opening the datasets behind our AI weather models

**What changed:** Google DeepMind Blog published the update on 10 Nov 2026. Google DeepMind's WeatherNext team released two datasets, SatWeave and PARDIG, openly on Google Cloud Storage, with PARDIG historical catalogs also in Google Earth Engine. SatWeave is a global mosaic of geostationary satellite imagery across 17 channels that updates hourly, with an archive spanning over a decade. PARDIG is an AI-based global precipitation dataset at 15-minute and roughly 10-kilometer resolution.

> Short source excerpt: Foundational precipitation and geostationary satellite datasets to observe the planet clearly. November 13, 2026 Science Opening the datasets behind our…

**Why it matters:** According to the source, SatWeave's hourly updates let WeatherNext 3 produce new global predictions, unlike models initialized on analysis data refreshed every six hours. PARDIG improves hourly precipitation detection skill over NASA's IMERG-Final by more than 30% on light-to-moderate precipitation, and the team expects reliable accuracy even where ground measurements are unavailable. Both are aimed at researchers and developers.

**How to verify it:** Open Google Cloud Storage and check that the SatWeave live feed and its archive of over a decade and the PARDIG live feed and 1999–2026 archives are listed; the source also says PARDIG historical catalogs are in Google Earth Engine.

**Uncertainty:** The text does not state licence terms, any cost for accessing or downloading the data, or which regions the live feeds cover. It also describes SatWeave as combining five satellites and, later, six geostationary satellites.
