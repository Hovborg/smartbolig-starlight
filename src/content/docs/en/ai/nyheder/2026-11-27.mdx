### 2. OpenAI News: Searching for distant planets with Astra

**What changed:** OpenAI News published the update on 24 Nov 2026. Two amateur astronomers used Codex and Astra to search public telescope data from NASA's Kepler and TESS missions for signs of planets. Kevin Bond used Codex on observations of about 100,000 Kepler stars; Ethan Byrd used Astra on data from about 19 million stars. Both searches identified potential exoplanets, which they have passed to professional astronomers.

> Short source excerpt: See how two amateur astronomers used Codex and Astra to find potential exoplanets in public data from NASA’s Kepler and…

**Why it matters:** The source describes this as amateur astronomers designing and running searches of their own, often on data that has already yielded the more obvious exoplanet signals. Bond's search flagged more than 200 signals, including 10 from previously confirmed planets.

**How to verify it:** The source names the two stars Bond's search singled out, KIC 5689121 and KIC 6691030, with dips every 13.8 days and every 4.8 days. A reader can look up those identifiers in the public Kepler data the source describes.

**Uncertainty:** The source does not say whether the candidates are confirmed planets. It also does not say when the TESS candidate will be analysed, and the provided text is truncated before the end.
