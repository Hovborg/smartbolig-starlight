This page explains what SmartBolig.net stores in your browser, who does it, and how you say yes or no.

---

## In short

- Until you have answered the consent message, the site fetches neither Google Analytics nor Google AdSense.
- If you say no, they are not fetched and set no cookies.
- You can change your answer at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.
- The comments under the guides are fetched only when you press **Show comments**.

---

## What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. The rules also cover similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage. This page covers both.

---

## What the site stores without asking

These are needed for the site to work, or to remember a choice you made yourself. They are not used for statistics or advertising.

| Name | Who | Purpose | Lifetime |
|------|-----|---------|----------|
| `FCCDCF` | Google's consent message | Remembers your answer to the consent message | 13 months |
| `FCNEC` | Google's consent message | May be set by the consent message; Google states its purpose as analytics | 365 days |
| `starlight-theme` (local storage) | SmartBolig.net | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you delete it |
| `smartbolig-motion-v1` (local storage) | SmartBolig.net | Remembers whether you switched off motion on the homepage | Until you delete it |
| The conversation with SmartBolig AI (session storage) | SmartBolig.net | So the conversation survives a reload | Until you close the tab or press **New conversation** |

Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set the cookie `__cf_bm` (expires after 30 minutes of inactivity) or `cf_clearance` if a visit has to be checked as a possible bot. On an ordinary visit the site itself sets no cookies (measured 14 November 2026).

The site also uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to count page views and measure load time. Cloudflare states that the service uses no cookies or local storage and does not recognise individual visitors across sites.

## Third parties

### Google (consent message, Analytics and AdSense)
- **The consent message** is Google's own (AdSense, Privacy & messaging) and follows the IAB consent standard (TCF). It is fetched from `fundingchoicesmessages.google.com` on every page.
- **Google Analytics** measures which pages are read. Fetched only after a yes to statistics.
- **Google AdSense** shows ads. Fetched only after a yes to advertising. Publisher ID: ca-pub-1259715054941263.
- [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Cloudflare
- Delivers the site, protects it against abuse and runs SmartBolig AI.
- [Cloudflare's cookies](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) and [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)

### Giscus and GitHub (comments)
- Fetched from `giscus.app`, only when you press **Show comments**.
- Giscus states that the service uses no cookies. If you sign in with GitHub to write a comment, an encrypted sign-in token is stored in the browser's local storage.
- [Giscus' privacy policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md)

### Partner-Ads (advertising links)
- When you press an advertising link, you are sent through Partner-Ads to the shop, so the shop can see that the visit came from SmartBolig.net. That happens at Partner-Ads and the shop. SmartBolig.net sets no cookie for it.
- [Partner-Ads' personal data policy](https://www.partner-ads.com/dk/persondatapolitik.php) (in Danish)

## How to change your answer

### On the site
Press **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page. The consent message opens again, and you can say yes or no to each purpose. If you withdraw a yes, the page is loaded again without the scripts you said no to.

### In your browser
You can delete or block cookies in the browser's settings. That also removes cookies that are already set:
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
