## 2. What information do we collect?

### 2.1 Information you provide
- Email address and the content of your message, if you write to us
- Comments you write under a guide (they are stored at GitHub, see section 5)

### 2.2 Automatically collected information
- IP address (Cloudflare receives it on every visit as part of operations and security; Google receives it when your browser fetches the consent message and, after your yes, Analytics and ads)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Pages visited and time of visit
- Referring website

### 2.3 AI assistant

When you actively send a question to SmartBolig AI, we process:

- Your question and up to the 10 most recent messages in the current conversation
- Your selected language (Danish or English)
- Your IP address through Cloudflare rate limiting to reduce abuse and unexpected AI costs

The question is processed by **Cloudflare Workers AI**. The assistant may also
search SmartBolig.net's own public pages when a relevant guide can improve the
answer. The search runs in an index stored with the site at Cloudflare; your
question is not sent to a separate search service. General questions can be
answered without this search.

SmartBolig.net does not create a user account or server-side conversation
history for the chat. The latest conversation is stored only in the browser's
`sessionStorage`, allowing it to survive a reload in the same tab session. You
can delete it with **New chat**, and it is normally cleared when the tab session
ends. Do not enter passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys or sensitive
personal data in the chat.

## 4. Cookies and tracking

Google's consent message asks whether you allow statistics and advertising. Until you have answered, the site fetches neither Google Analytics nor Google AdSense. That also holds if you are in a country where Google has to ask but the message is not shown to you. If you say no, they are not fetched.

Google shows the question only in countries where the rules require it (the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). If you visit from another country, Google Analytics and Google AdSense may be fetched without a question.

- **Necessary:** Your answer to the consent message and your own choices of theme and motion
- **Statistics (only with your yes):** Google Analytics
- **Advertising (only with your yes):** Google AdSense

Once you have answered, you can change your answer with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of the page. All cookies and their lifetimes are listed in our [cookie policy](/en/juridisk/cookiepolitik/).

## 5. Data sharing

We use the following services for analytics, advertising and operation:
- **Google Analytics** - Traffic analysis (only with your consent)
- **Google AdSense** - Advertising (only with your consent)
- **Google's consent message** - Shows the question about cookies and remembers your answer
- **Cloudflare** - Hosting, security, CDN, Workers AI and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies
- **Partner-Ads** - Registers clicks on advertising links and any purchases (see section 11)

Google is itself the data controller for Google AdSense and for the consent message; Google's controller terms and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply there. For Google Analytics, Google is our data processor under Google's data processing terms, and Cloudflare's data processing addendum applies to Cloudflare. Transfers to the US rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

Cloudflare states in its [Workers AI data usage documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/)
that customer inputs and outputs are not used to train the available AI models
or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent.
Cloudflare's current terms and privacy policy apply to its processing.

Under the guides you can open the **Giscus** comment feature, which uses
**GitHub Discussions**. Your browser contacts `giscus.app` only when you press
**Show comments**. Sign-in and comments are handled through GitHub. Read the
[Giscus privacy policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md)
and [GitHub privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
before signing in or posting a comment.

## 8. Data retention

We keep data for as long as it is necessary for the purpose, or as required by law. Analytics data in Google Analytics is retained for up to 14 months.

SmartBolig.net does not write chat questions or AI answers to its own database
or application logs. The local browser copy follows the tab session as
described in section 2.3. Cloudflare processes chat content and operational
metadata under its current product terms and policies; deleting browser
`sessionStorage` therefore does not guarantee a specific deletion time in
Cloudflare's systems.

## 11. Affiliate links and recommendations

SmartBolig.net participates in affiliate programs, which means we may receive commission when you buy products via links on our site.

### How it works
- We link to products we use or recommend ourselves
- If you buy via our links, we receive a small commission
- **It costs you nothing extra** - the price is the same

### Our affiliate partners
- Proshop.dk
- Specialkamera.dk
- RobotterOnline.dk
- WATTOO.DK
- Batteribyen.dk

The current partner list is always available in our [affiliate disclosure](/en/juridisk/affiliate-disclosure/).

The agreements are managed through **Partner-Ads**. When you press an advertising link, you are sent through Partner-Ads to the shop, and Partner-Ads registers the click and any purchase so the commission can be calculated. See [Partner-Ads' personal data policy](https://www.partner-ads.com/dk/persondatapolitik.php) (in Danish).

### Our commitment
- We only recommend products we would use ourselves
- Affiliate revenue does **not** influence our recommendations
- We always disclose when a page contains affiliate links
