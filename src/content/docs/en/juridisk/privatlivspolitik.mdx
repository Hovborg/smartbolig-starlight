## 2. What information do we collect?

### 2.1 Information you provide
- Email address (if you sign up for the newsletter)
- Comments or messages you send

### 2.2 Automatically collected information
- IP address (anonymized in Google Analytics; Cloudflare and advertising partners process the full IP address as part of operations and security)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Pages visited and time of visit
- Referring website

### 2.3 AI assistant

When you actively send a question to SmartBolig AI, we process:

- Your question and up to the 10 most recent messages in the current conversation
- Your selected language (Danish or English)
- Your IP address through Cloudflare rate limiting to reduce abuse and unexpected AI costs

The question is processed by **Cloudflare Workers AI**. The assistant may also
search SmartBolig.net's public content through **Cloudflare AI Search** when a
relevant guide can improve the answer. AI Search is a supplementary source
tool; general questions can be answered without this search.

SmartBolig.net does not create a user account or server-side conversation
history for the chat. The latest conversation is stored only in the browser's
`sessionStorage`, allowing it to survive a reload in the same tab session. You
can delete it with **New chat**, and it is normally cleared when the tab session
ends. Do not enter passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys or sensitive
personal data in the chat.

We collect data to:
- Improve our website and content
- Analyze traffic and user behavior

**Legal basis:** Analytical and marketing cookies are only used with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Operating and securing the website, responding to inquiries, and providing and protecting the user-activated AI assistant are based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

## 5. Data sharing

We only share data with:
- **Google Analytics** - Traffic analysis
- **Google AdSense** - Advertising
- **Ezoic** - Advertising and optimization
- **Cloudflare** - Hosting, security, CDN, Workers AI and AI Search

Processing by these providers is governed by data processing agreements, and transfers to the US rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

Cloudflare states in its [Workers AI data usage documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/)
that customer inputs and outputs are not used to train the available AI models
or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent.
Cloudflare's current terms and privacy policy apply to its processing.

## 6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:
- **Access:** See what data we hold about you
- **Rectification:** Have incorrect information corrected
- **Erasure:** Have your data deleted
- **Restriction:** Limit the processing of your data
- **Objection:** Object to processing
- **Data portability:** Receive a copy of your data

Contact us at kontakt@smartbolig.net to exercise your rights.

## 7. Data security

We protect your data with:
- SSL/TLS encryption
- Secure hosting via Cloudflare
- Regular security updates

## 8. Data retention

We keep data for as long as it is necessary for the purpose, or as required by law. Anonymized analytics data in Google Analytics is retained for up to 14 months.

SmartBolig.net does not write chat questions or AI answers to its own database
or application logs. The local browser copy follows the tab session as
described in section 2.3. Cloudflare processes chat content and operational
metadata under its current product terms and policies; deleting browser
`sessionStorage` therefore does not guarantee a specific deletion time in
Cloudflare's systems.
