Here you find small tools that do the sums or the choosing for you. They work right in the browser, and you do not need to sign up for anything.

<ToolCards lang="en" />

## How the tools are made

- **You can see the sum.** Each tool explains how the number is calculated or the kit is chosen, and what is not included.
- **We do not store what you type.** The numbers are calculated in your browser. The tools use neither cookies nor the browser's storage.
- **The source is on the page.** The electricity prices come from Energinet, and the tool says so.

## From numbers to action

A number is worth most when it changes something at home. These guides take the next step:

- [Electricity price integration](/en/home-assistant/elpris-integration/): get the electricity prices into Home Assistant.
- [Run appliances in the cheapest hours](/en/automationer/billige-timer/): let an automation start the appliance when electricity is cheapest.
- [Energy management and monitoring](/en/produkter/energistyring/): measure what your appliances really use.
