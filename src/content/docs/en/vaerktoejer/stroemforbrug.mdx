Type how many watts an appliance uses and how long it is on each day. You then see what it costs in electricity per day, month and year.

<PowerCost lang="en" />

## How the number is calculated

The tool multiplies the power by the time and the price:

**watts ÷ 1000 × hours per day × price per kWh = kroner per day**

A month is counted as 30.4 days and a year as 365 days. Everything is calculated in your browser, and we do not store what you type.

## What is not included

- **The draw varies.** A fridge and a dehumidifier switch on and off by themselves, and a PC uses more when it is working. Use an average, or measure the consumption.
- **The price varies.** The electricity price changes hour by hour. The tool uses the same price all year.
- **Fixed amounts.** Subscription and other fixed amounts on the electricity bill are not included. They do not change when you switch an appliance off.

## Find the right numbers

- The power is printed on the appliance's rating plate. That is often the highest draw and not the typical one.
- A smart plug with a meter shows the real consumption. See [Energy management and monitoring](/en/produkter/energistyring/).
- Your price per kWh is on your electricity bill. The spot price right now is in [the electricity price tool](/en/vaerktoejer/elpris/).
- To follow the consumption over time, use the [Home Assistant Energy Dashboard](/en/home-assistant/energy-dashboard/).
