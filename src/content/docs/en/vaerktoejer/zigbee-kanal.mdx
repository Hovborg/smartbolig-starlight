## How the number is calculated

Zigbee uses 16 channels, numbered 11 to 26. Channel 11 sits at 2405 MHz, and the centres of two channels are 5 MHz apart. Each channel is 2 MHz wide. Wi-Fi channel 1 sits at 2412 MHz and channel 11 at 2462 MHz; the tool shows channels 1 to 13.

The tool counts a Wi-Fi channel as 22 MHz wide, the widest common mask. That is our choice, not a figure from a source, and it is cautious: a narrower Wi-Fi channel takes up less. A Zigbee channel overlaps when the two bands touch at more than a single point. The best Zigbee channel is the one whose centre is furthest from the edge of a marked Wi-Fi band. On a tie, 15, 20, 25 and 11 are picked in that order, and then the lowest number.

Channel 26 is never picked first. We have not found an official source that says why that channel is worse, so this is a cautious rule and not a claim. ZHA only recommends 15, 20 and 25, because not all Zigbee devices support all channels.

## What is not included

- **Signal strength.** The tool only knows the frequencies. A neighbour's Wi-Fi that is weak at your home disturbs less than the bar suggests.
- **Other things on 2.4 GHz.** Zigbee2MQTT mentions that Bluetooth and wireless gaming accessories, among others, can disturb Zigbee. The tool cannot see them.
- **What your devices can do.** Not all devices support all channels. See "Change the channel" below.

## Change the channel

The two systems document this differently, and both deserve to be taken seriously.

**Zigbee2MQTT** has channel 11 as its default and says to use a ZLL channel to avoid problems: 11, 15, 20 or 25. Its documentation says changing the channel of an existing network is supported. Devices that are asleep (often battery powered) switch only when they next wake up, so you should wake them after the change. Some devices cannot change channel; if one does not answer after several minutes, you may have to pair it again. Changing the channel only works with the `zstack` and `ember` adapters.

**ZHA** (Home Assistant's own Zigbee) says it is best not to change the channel from the default. The "Smart" option scans the channels and picks one, preferring 15, 20 or 25. After a change it can take up to an hour before all devices have found the new channel; you can speed it up by power cycling the end devices. If you have trouble with overlap, ZHA says it is usually better to first give the Wi-Fi router a fixed channel.

You can also move Wi-Fi instead of Zigbee. If you move the router's channel, mark the new channel above and see whether the recommendation changes.
