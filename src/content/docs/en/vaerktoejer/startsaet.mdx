## How the kit is chosen

A kit has three layers:

1. **The brain** is Home Assistant, which gathers all the parts in one place. If you already have it, there is nothing to buy in that layer.
2. **The connection** is how the parts talk to the brain. Zigbee parts need a Zigbee coordinator, and Wi-Fi parts use your router. The coordinator is only included when the kit has Zigbee parts.
3. **The parts for your goals** come as one block for each goal you choose.

The rules are the same for everyone:

- **Tenant** gives you only parts you can take with you when you move: bulbs, plugs and battery-powered sensors. Nothing behind a switch or in the consumer unit (fuse box).
- **Owner** gives you the same parts plus a dimmer behind the wall switch when you choose light. The dimmer becomes part of the fixed installation, so an authorised electrician has to fit it.
- **The same sensor is listed in one place only.** If you choose both light and security, they share one sensor.
- **The first steps follow the kit.** You get three to five steps, and the last one gets the first of your goals working, in the order the goals are shown above.

## What is not included

- **Prices.** They change, and the shop shows the current one. Nor do we say that a part is the cheapest or the best.
- **Parts without a buying recommendation.** We do not yet have a buying recommendation for the computer Home Assistant runs on, for a reader for the electricity meter or for ESP32 parts. The kit says so and links to the guide.
- **Your bridge.** If you have a Zigbee bridge from, say, Hue or IKEA, you can keep it: a Hue bridge can control Hue bulbs. We have not checked which of the kit's other parts your bridge can control. So the kit is built for Home Assistant with a Zigbee coordinator.
- **Alarm and cameras.** The Security block sends a message to your phone. It is not an alarm with a siren, and there is no monitoring centre behind it.
- **Your home.** The tool does not know your radiators, your lamps or your lease. Check that a part fits before you buy it.

## Where the parts come from

Every product in the kit appears in the site's guides. The shop links are the ones the Danish guides use, and they go to a Danish shop, Proshop. They are ad links: we may receive a commission if you buy through them. [Read more about ad links](/en/juridisk/affiliate-disclosure/).

The kit is chosen in your browser. We do not store your answers, and they are not sent anywhere.
